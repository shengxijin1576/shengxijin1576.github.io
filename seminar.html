Symplectic Topology Student Seminar (Fall 2026)

Topic: Microlocal sheaf theory and Fukaya categories

Organizers: Shengxi Jin and Frank Zheng

Description: Following the pioneering work of Nadler-Zaslow, Ganatra-Pardon-Shende relates the partially wrapped Fukaya category of a Weinstein manifold to a category of certain sheaves on its skeleton. In this seminar, we will study the microlocal theory of sheaves and its applications to symplectic geometry and contact geometry.
