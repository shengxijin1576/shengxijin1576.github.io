Symplectic Topology Student Seminar (Fall 2026)

Topic: Microlocal sheaf theory and Fukaya categories

Organizers: Shengxi Jin and Yichen Cheng

Description: Following the pioneering work of Nadler-Zaslow, Ganatra-Pardon-Shende relates the partially wrapped Fukaya category of a Weinstein manifold to a category of certain sheaves on its skeleton. In this seminar, we will study the microlocal theory of sheaves and its applications to symplectic geometry and contact geometry.

We will discuss the classical approach (going back to Grothendieck) to defining the derived category of sheaves on a topological space. Our goal will be to define several "derived" functors associated to a continuous map f of topological spaces: the pushforward f_*, the pullback f^*, the proper pushforward f_!, and the proper pullback f^!. In order to do this, we will discuss some of the general machinery for defining derived categories and functors given an arbitrary abelian category.
